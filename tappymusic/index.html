How it plays

Tap anywhere on the screen and the next note of the melody sounds. Tap fast, tap slow, stop halfway and start again: the tune is always right, and the rhythm is always theirs.

No keys to miss

There is no target to hit and no wrong note to play. Anywhere is correct.

Two hands, if you like

The top of the screen plays the melody; the bottom plays it lower, like a left hand. Both at once plays both.

Every tap blooms

Clipart chosen by the song bursts wherever a finger landed, and louder notes throw more of it.

No ads. No subscriptions. No accounts. No analytics, no tracking, and no data leaves the device — the only server the app ever talks to is the App Store. It works completely offline. Purchases are one-time and Family Shareable.

What is inside

128 melodies across 14 packs — nursery rhymes, bedtime songs, classical, folk and campfire, holiday music, and one pack per tradition. All public domain, played through a real General MIDI sampler on 83 instruments.

First Taps — ten nursery favourites — is free and complete. Every paid pack plays one melody free, so nothing is bought unheard.

Songs from home

Eight packs, each from one tradition: French chansons, German folk songs, Italian tarantellas, Spanish jotas, Russian folk, Ukrainian hopaks, Greek dances and Klezmer. For a family living far from where it began, these are the songs a grandparent sang — played by a grandchild's own hands.

Learning to play it properly

When a child wants more than freedom, the learning menu is there. None of it is compulsory, and none of it is ever shown as a score out of ten.

Follow along

A ball bounces and lands exactly when the next note is due, falling under gravity — so the rhythm can be seen before it arrives.

Listen

Hear how the song goes from here, at full speed or half, one phrase at a time.

See how you played

At the end, a picture of how closely the playing kept to the written rhythm. A picture, not a mark.

Questions parents ask

What age is it for?

Children up to five, and well past it: the learning tools are there for the day a child wants to play a song properly.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline. The only server the app ever contacts is the App Store, for purchases.

Is it a piano app?

There is no keyboard. The whole screen is the instrument, and every song plays on its own sound — music box, xylophone, cello, piano and many more.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. The shop and every purchase sit behind a parental gate that a young child cannot read.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, on iPhone and iPad. Turn an iPad sideways and the left side plays the song lower, for two hands.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase is one-time and yours to keep, and Family Sharing covers the whole family.

The app speaks eleven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Greek, Ukrainian and Russian.
