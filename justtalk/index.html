Dictate into any app

JustTalk is a keyboard for iPhone and iPad. Tap the microphone in any app — Messages, Mail, WhatsApp, Notes, your AI chat — speak, and the text appears where your cursor is, already cleaned up: no ums, sentences punctuated, your corrections taken into account.

Speak, and it is written

  • The microphone sits on the letters. Dictate in the middle of typing, without switching keyboards.
  • Your words appear as you speak, and each sentence is typed polished as soon as you pause.
  • Corrections win. “Tuesday — no, Thursday” is typed as Thursday.
  • Start from anywhere: the Action button, Control Centre or Back Tap starts a session without opening the app.
  • Nothing lost: if the connection drops, your iPhone's own recognition fills in, and the last dictation can be typed again with one tap for ten minutes.

A full keyboard, too

  • Word suggestions above the letters, and the next word before you type it.
  • Fix my typing corrects typos and punctuation when you pause — never your wording.
  • Correct a name once and JustTalk offers to remember it, so it is spelled right next time.
  • Emoji, cursor keys, undo and redo, in light and dark.

On Mac and Windows: one hotkey

Hold the hotkey, speak, let go: the polished text is pasted into the app in front of you — the mail you are writing, the document, the terminal, the chat. A small capsule shows that it is listening and nothing else gets in your way. The same modes, phrases and actions as on the phone, with a palette for phrases and actions that opens on a key.

Modes: the same words, written for where they go

One tap changes how your dictation is written

A mode is an instruction for the polish. Choose one on the keyboard or in the menu, and every dictation comes out in its style:

  • General — your words, cleaned up. Nothing added, nothing rephrased.
  • Official — formal and polite, for a letter to a landlord, a bank or an authority.
  • Email — the body of an email, in your own voice.
  • Social — a post for your feed, with emoji and hashtags.
  • AI Prompt — a clear prompt for ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini: the task first, then context and requirements.
  • Translate — speak one language, type another.

Write your own modes, or let the AI write the instruction from a sentence. Test this mode runs six sample dictations in each of your languages and shows in plain words what went wrong. On Mac and Windows the right mode follows the app — Email in Mail or Outlook, AI Prompt in ChatGPT; on iPhone a Shortcuts automation does the same.

Speak one language, type another

Translation as you dictate

  • Choose Translate and the language to type in. Speak any of your languages; the text arrives translated, cleaned up like any dictation.
  • Names, numbers and addresses stay as you said them, and your tone stays yours — a quick message stays a quick message.
  • The keyboard shows the language it types in, and afterwards which language it translated from.
  • On the Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad alike.

Phrases: what you write again and again

One tap, one shortcut, or one spoken word

  • Your address, your bank details, a standard reply, a signature: saved once, inserted with one tap from the keyboard or the palette.
  • Text shortcuts: give your address the shortcut “adr”, type it and a space, and the address is there. JustTalk suggests your phrases as you type.
  • Say to insert: a word you speak in a dictation becomes the phrase.
  • Groups as tabs, pinned phrases first, then the ones you use most. Starter sets to begin with.

Actions: rewrite what you selected

Your own instructions, on any text

  • Select text — or nothing, for the whole field — and tap an action: shorter, friendlier, as a list, fix the grammar, into English.
  • Write an action in your own words, or describe it and let the AI write the instruction.
  • The result replaces the text in place, and one undo brings the original back.
  • From the keyboard on iPhone and iPad, from the palette on Mac and Windows.

JustTalk Notes: meetings, voice notes and tasks

Minutes you did not have to write

  • Meetings: on Mac and Windows both sides of a call, on iPhone and iPad a meeting in the room. A transcript as people speak, then the minutes: summary, decisions, action items.
  • Voice notes: speak a thought, get it written up in the format you chose.
  • Tasks from every meeting in one list — who does what, by when — and what is still open from last time when the same people meet again.
  • Ask a folder a question: the answer cites the notes it came from, and every point links to the moment it was said.
  • Folders, tags, search, export to PDF, Word and Markdown; on Apple devices kept in step through iCloud, if you switch it on.

Your words stay yours

Nothing you type or say is ever sent to us.

On iPhone and iPad the keyboard asks for Full Access. Full Access is what lets the JustTalk keyboard receive what the JustTalk app hears, and reach the service you chose. Without it, a keyboard can only type letters.

  • Never sent to us. No server, no analytics, no account.
  • Dictation goes only to the service you chose, or stays on your device.
  • Password fields always get Apple's keyboard.
  • What you dictate is not kept.

Check it yourself: on your iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report — and in JustTalk, Settings → Privacy → What left your iPhone, which services JustTalk contacted, with none of your words in it.

What it costs

The app is one payment. The models are free if you run them locally, and pay-as-you-go if you use a cloud provider — billed by them, not by us. Ten minutes of dictation a day on Groq is well under a dollar a month at their September 2026 rates; a heavy 45 minutes a day is roughly one to three dollars. Everything is unlocked for seven days, with nothing held back and no card required.

After the trial the free version keeps working, every day: 2 dictations of up to 30 characters each, 5 phrases, 5 actions and, on iPhone and iPad, 5 typing fixes. JustTalk Pro makes all of it unlimited for a single payment — with a cloud AI service, use is then limited only by your access to that service. It is not a subscription, and there is nothing to cancel.

Local or cloud — you decide, and we say what each costs

JustTalk is the app around the models, not the models. Run them locally — on the device, or on a computer with a model server such as Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp — and nothing is uploaded and nothing is billed. Or connect a cloud provider with a key you create: faster and more accurate, and they bill you directly. The privacy policy says exactly what each option sends, and the AI statement which AI does what. How accurate, polished and fast the results are depends on the models you choose and on the modes and actions you give them — switch either at any time; the terms say more.

Made to the EU AI Act's transparency rules. Nothing goes to an AI service until you allow it, and minutes, summaries and other text an AI writes are marked as AI-written in the app and in every export. Dictation is the one exception: it is your own words, polished and typed into another app, where no mark can travel with them. The AI statement explains it all.
