What it does

Dictation software usually hands you a transcript and leaves the tidying to you. JustTalk finishes the job: press a hotkey, speak, and the cleaned-up sentence is pasted into whatever app is already in front of you — no window to switch to, nothing to copy across.

Written, not transcribed

A language model strips the ums and fixes the punctuation. What lands in the field is something you would have typed.

On-device or cloud

Run Whisper locally and nothing leaves your Mac. Or point it at a cloud model when you want the accuracy. You choose, and you can change.

99 languages

Detected as you speak, including mid-sentence. No setting to flip when you switch.

What it costs

Everything is unlocked for seven days, with nothing held back and no card required.

After the trial the free version keeps working, with a daily limit on how much you can dictate. JustTalk Pro removes that limit for a single payment. It is not a subscription, and there is nothing to cancel.

How people use it

Email and chat

Modes carry their own tone and vocabulary, and the right one can be picked automatically from the app you are dictating into.

Editing by voice

Select something you already wrote and ask for a change — shorter, formal, as a list. The rewrite replaces the selection in place.

Meetings

Capture both sides of a call and get a transcript of the whole conversation rather than just your half.

What leaves your Mac is your choice

No account. No analytics. No tracking. JustTalk never phones home. It sends your speech only to the backend you chose — and with on-device Whisper selected, nothing leaves your Mac at all. The privacy policy says exactly what each option sends.

Dictate in 99 languages, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Greek, Ukrainian and Russian.
