Your words stay yours

Nothing you type or say is ever sent to us.

On iPhone and iPad the keyboard asks for Full Access. Full Access is what lets the JustTalk keyboard receive what the JustTalk app hears, and reach the service you chose. Without it, a keyboard can only type letters.

  • Never sent to us. No server, no analytics, no account.
  • Dictation goes only to the service you chose, or stays on your device.
  • Password fields always get Apple's keyboard.
  • What you dictate is not kept.

Check it yourself: on your iPhone, Settings → Privacy & Security → App Privacy Report — and in JustTalk, Settings → Privacy → What left your iPhone, which services JustTalk contacted, with none of your words in it.

What it does

Dictation software usually hands you a transcript and leaves the tidying to you. JustTalk finishes the job: press a hotkey, speak, and the cleaned-up sentence is pasted into whatever app is already in front of you — no window to switch to, nothing to copy across.

Written, not transcribed

A language model strips the ums and fixes the punctuation. What lands in the field is something you would have typed.

Local or cloud, your call

Whisper and a local model run entirely on your own computer, free and offline. Or point it at Google, OpenAI, Groq, Mistral or Anthropic with your own key and pay them directly.

100 languages

Detected as you speak, including mid-sentence. No setting to flip when you switch.

What it costs

The app is one payment. The models are free if you run them locally, and pay-as-you-go if you use a cloud provider — billed by them, not by us. Ten minutes of dictation a day on Groq is well under a dollar a month at their September 2026 rates; a heavy 45 minutes a day is roughly one to three dollars. Everything is unlocked for seven days, with nothing held back and no card required.

After the trial the free version keeps working, every day: 2 dictations of up to 30 characters each, 5 phrases, 5 actions and, on iPhone and iPad, 5 typing fixes. JustTalk Pro makes all of it unlimited for a single payment — with a cloud AI service, use is then limited only by your access to that service. It is not a subscription, and there is nothing to cancel.

How people use it

Email and chat

General handles everyday writing; the other modes carry their own tone and vocabulary. On Mac and Windows the right one follows the app out of the box — Email in Mail or Outlook, AI Prompt in ChatGPT or Claude; on iPhone and iPad a Shortcuts automation does the same.

Meetings

On Mac and Windows, capture both sides of a call; on iPhone and iPad, lay the device on the table for a meeting in the room. Either way you get a transcript of the whole conversation, and the minutes. Later, ask a whole folder of them a question: every point of the answer links to the moment it was said.

Phrases and Actions

What you write again and again is one tap away, on the iPhone keyboard and in the desktop palette. An Action rewrites the selection, or the whole field, with an instruction you write — or have the model write.

Recordings

A voice memo, a lecture, an interview: open or share it with JustTalk and get the transcript, on every platform.

Local or cloud — you decide, and we say what each costs

JustTalk is the app around the models, not the models. Run them locally — on the device, or on a desktop with Whisper and llama.cpp, Ollama or LM Studio — and nothing is uploaded and nothing is billed. Or connect a cloud provider with a key you create: faster and more accurate, and they bill you directly. The privacy policy says exactly what each option sends, and the AI statement which AI does what. How accurate, polished and fast the results are depends on the models you choose and on the modes and actions you give them — switch either at any time; the terms say more.

Made to the EU AI Act's transparency rules. Nothing goes to an AI service until you allow it, and minutes, summaries and other text an AI writes are marked as AI-written in the app and in every export. Dictation is the one exception: it is your own words, polished and typed into another app, where no mark can travel with them. The AI statement explains it all.
