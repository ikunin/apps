Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 September 2026. Applies to the JustTalk macOS app and this website.

JustTalk has no analytics, no crash reporter, no tracking and no account with us. It does not phone home. It does, in normal use, send your speech to a cloud transcription and polishing service that you choose and hold the key for — so most of what matters here is that provider's policy, not this one. The on-device option sends nothing anywhere, but it is the exception rather than the default.

What stays on your Mac

All of it, unless you pick a cloud backend. Stored locally and never uploaded by JustTalk:

  • Your dictation history, including the transcribed text
  • Modes, your custom dictionary and quick actions
  • Usage statistics — words dictated, time saved, estimated spend
  • Recorded audio, which is held in memory for the length of a dictation and not written to disk
  • API keys and sign-in tokens for the services you configure, in the macOS Keychain
  • Whisper models you download, if you use on-device transcription

Deleting the app's Application Support folder deletes all of it. JustTalk has no server to delete anything from.

Purchases

JustTalk Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. The App Store tells the app whether it is entitled; it never sees your name, your email or your payment details.

The purchase is Family Shareable. If a member of your family group shares it with you, Apple tells the app it is entitled and nothing else about them.

When you choose a cloud backend

JustTalk can send audio, or the transcript of it, to a provider you configure — Google, OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, Anthropic, or a server address you enter yourself. That data is then handled under their privacy policy, not this one, and JustTalk has no visibility into what they keep. The app shows which provider is active before you record, and switching to on-device Whisper stops any of it from leaving your Mac.

What is sent alongside your words

When a polishing model is in use, JustTalk may include the name of the app you are dictating into so the result fits the context. You can turn that off with Privacy Mode, which stops any app or window information being used or sent.

Children

JustTalk is a productivity tool for adults, not a children's app. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Required-reason APIs

JustTalk uses the microphone, and system audio when you record a meeting, and it asks macOS for permission before either. It reads no contacts, no location, no photos and no health data. Apple's privacy questionnaire for the listing is answered from exactly this page.

This website

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. They set no cookies, embed no scripts and run no analytics. GitHub may log requests as part of serving them — see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that reduced your privacy would come with a new version of the app, not a quiet edit here.
