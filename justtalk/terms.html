All versions

The free version and JustTalk Pro

Every version is fully unlocked for a seven-day trial, then keeps working as a free version with a daily limit. Unlocking it for good is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. How each platform sells it is in its part.

The purchase removes the daily limit and the length cap on dictated text. It does not unlock features held back from the free version — every backend, mode, dictionary and quick action is available without it.

Third-party AI services, and who pays for them

JustTalk does not include the speech and language models it uses. You choose where they run. If you choose a cloud service — Google, OpenAI, Groq, Mistral, Anthropic, OpenRouter or any other endpoint you configure — you are the customer of that provider, not of us. You create the account, you hold the API key, and that provider charges you directly for everything the app sends on your behalf. Those charges are additional to the price of this app. They are governed by your agreement with that provider, and we cannot see them, cap them, waive them or refund them. Usage grows with how much you dictate and with the model you pick, so check your provider's pricing and set a spending limit there if you want one. Running the models locally instead costs nothing beyond your own hardware.

Your words, and what you point the app at

What you dictate is yours. If you configure a cloud backend, you are responsible for having the right to send that content to the provider you chose, and for complying with their terms. Keys you enter are yours to manage.

The models the app can use

JustTalk ships no speech or language model of its own. Optional local language models are downloaded from their own publishers and are covered by their own licences, which the app shows under Acknowledgements. Which on-device speech recogniser each version uses is in its part.

What the app does not do

It shows no advertising, contains no social features, and makes no network request that you have not configured. See the privacy policy.

No warranty

The app is provided as is. Transcription and polishing are performed by statistical models and will sometimes be wrong; check anything that matters before you send it. To the extent permitted by law, and without limiting any rights you have as a consumer, no warranty is given.
