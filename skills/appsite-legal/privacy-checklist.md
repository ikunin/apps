# Privacy policy checklist

Tick each against the fact sheet; a line that does not apply says so in the
policy only if a reader would otherwise wonder ("no account", "no crash
reporter").

## GDPR Art. 13 (the licensor is in the EU)

- [ ] Controller: name, address, contact email (the Impressum block).
- [ ] Each processing the developer itself does (support email, licence
      requests, anything else that reaches us): purpose and legal basis
      (Art. 6(1)(b) contract/request, (c) legal duty such as tax records,
      (a) consent, (f) legitimate interest — name the interest).
- [ ] Recipients: the mail provider and any other processor.
- [ ] **Transfers outside the EU/EEA** and their safeguard (a US-based mail
      provider: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual
      clauses). Missing this is the most common gap.
- [ ] Retention: how long each kind is kept, or the rule that decides it.
- [ ] Rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability,
      objection, withdrawal of consent; the complaint authority (the user's
      own, or the licensor's — Berlin: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz
      und Informationsfreiheit).
- [ ] Whether providing data is required (support: only to get an answer).
- [ ] No automated decision-making (say so for an AI app).

## What the app itself does (no developer server ≠ nothing to say)

- [ ] Stored on the device: what, how long, how to delete; backups (system
      backups keep copies until they expire).
- [ ] Sync: through the user's own iCloud, what is end-to-end encrypted,
      on/off, what turning it off does.
- [ ] Every connection the app makes, by name, and what each sees (an IP
      address at least).
- [ ] Third-party services the user chooses (AI providers, integrations):
      the user is their customer, their policy applies, what is sent,
      consent first and where to revoke it.
- [ ] Apple's opt-in crash reports / analytics shared with developers.
- [ ] Permissions requested and why (microphone, speech, camera, calendar,
      local network, Face ID); what is never read.
- [ ] Children and the age rating.
- [ ] The site itself (static pages, no cookies, the host's logs).

## Apple

- [ ] App Review 5.1.1(i): the policy says what is collected, how, all uses,
      third parties that receive it (and that they protect it equally),
      retention and deletion, and how to revoke consent or request deletion.
- [ ] 5.1.2(i): personal data shared with a third party — **third-party AI
      included** — is disclosed by name and asked for first.
- [ ] The App Privacy label ("Data Not Collected" or the real categories) and
      every target's `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` say the same as the policy.
- [ ] The listing's privacy URL resolves to this page in every locale.

## Every surface says the same

- [ ] In-app privacy page, store descriptions, review notes, AI statement:
      grep for each fact that changed.
- [ ] `updated` bumped in all eleven languages.
