# Apple's Standard EULA and minimum terms — what our terms must say

Re-read the live EULA each time
(https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/); this is
the October 2026 reading.

## The Standard EULA, clause by clause

| Apple | What it says | Ours (Apple-sold apps: the EULA applies in full, ours adds) |
|---|---|---|
| preamble | licensed, not sold; Licensor reserves all rights | §2 "licensed, not sold, under Apple's Standard EULA, which applies in full; these terms add to it, and where the two differ, these terms apply between you and us"; §3 "the apps, their name and design remain ours and our licensors'" |
| a. Scope | devices you own/control per Usage Rules; no transfer, redistribution, reverse-engineering except law/open source | §3, plus: no getting around the free limits or the purchase check; open-source licences named by the app's own label |
| b. Consent to data | Licensor may collect technical data | we collect none — say what really reaches us (support email; Apple's opt-in crash reports) and point to the privacy policy |
| c. Termination | ends on breach | §16: user may stop any time; right ends on serious breach; user's data stays on their devices |
| d. External services | at your risk; not responsible for third-party content; info is general; no harassment/defamation; may change or remove | §9 connected services (name them all); §8 "what AI writes is general information, not professional advice"; §10 lawful use |
| e. No warranty | as is, as available, all faults; consumer laws may differ | §14 in the EU-safe form: statutory rights unaffected, then "as they are and as available", no further guarantee incl. AI accuracy |
| f. Liability | no incidental/consequential; $50 cap | §15 German form (unlimited for intent, gross negligence, life/body/health, product liability; slight negligence → essential obligations, foreseeable damage) |
| g. Export | US embargo, denied-party lists, prohibited uses | §10 one sentence "as the EULA sets out", and §2 "export restrictions apply as written" |
| h. US Government end users | Commercial Items | §2 "applies as written" |
| i. Governing law | California; EU/CH/NO/IS residents: home law and courts; CISG excluded | §17 ours: licensor's law, CISG excluded, EU/EEA/CH consumers' home protections and courts, merchants at licensor's courts, VSBG statement |

## Apple's minimum terms for a developer's EULA (state them; harmless with the Standard EULA)

| Requirement | Sentence |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement: agreement is between user and developer, not Apple | §2 |
| Maintenance and support: developer's, not Apple's | §2 + §20 support page |
| Warranty: Apple has none | §2 |
| Product claims (liability, consumer protection, legal compliance): developer's | §2 |
| Intellectual property claims: developer's | §2 "third-party intellectual property" |
| Legal compliance: not in an embargoed country / on a list | §10 |
| Developer name, address, contact | §1 + Impressum link, §20 |
| Third-party terms (e.g. the AI provider, iCloud) | §7, §9 |
| Third-party beneficiary: Apple may enforce | §2 |

## Beyond Apple — what an AI/notes app adds

- Costs of the user's own AI provider: you are their customer; we cannot see,
  cap, waive or refund; set a spending limit there.
- Results depend on the models **and the instructions** (modes, actions the
  user can change or write).
- Content and backups: we hold no copy; back up; a lock whose recovery secret
  is lost cannot be opened by anyone.
- EU AI Act: not for high-risk uses; Art. 50(4) disclosure duty when the user
  publishes AI text.
- Family Sharing, restore, price changes, withdrawal via Apple.
- TestFlight builds are pre-release.
