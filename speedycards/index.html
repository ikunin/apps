SpeedyCards is a fast, elegant solitaire-style card race for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV, and Apple Watch. Clear the board by spotting combos — a lone 10, a pair summing to ten, or Jack-Queen-King — as new cards deal in relentlessly. Race the clock, the deck, or your friends on the identical deal.

Spot the combo before the deck buries you

Clear the board by finding a lone 10, a pair that sums to ten, or a Jack-Queen-King. New cards deal in while you look, and they do not wait.

Three shapes, one glance

A ten on its own, two cards making ten, or the three faces together. That is the whole rulebook, and it is the last time you will think about it.

The deck keeps coming

Cards deal in on a timer. Clear faster than they arrive and the board empties; hesitate and it fills.

Race whatever you like

The clock, the deck, the Daily deal, or a friend on the identical shuffle.

The same shuffle, both players

A duel deals the identical cards to both of you, so the only difference is what you saw and how fast. Send a challenge link or a five-letter code over any chat app and race their ghost, clear by clear.

No account, no sign-up, no server of ours in the middle — the challenge is the code.

A reason to come back

One Daily deal, worldwide

Everyone gets the same board each day. Streaks carry over, and you can earn freezes for the day life gets in the way.

A weekly Gauntlet

A tournament on Game Center, run every week.

A Brass Album of thirty tables

Table themes earned by playing, not bought — with a Table Rank that grows every round, win or lose.

Five platforms, one game

It plays on the phone in your pocket, the Mac on your desk, the television across the room and the watch on your wrist. Streaks and progress follow you through your own iCloud, which we cannot read.

The board, wherever you are

Portrait on a phone, landscape on a Mac, across the room on a television, and a glance on the watch. Same deal, same rules, same run at your record.

Progress travels through your own iCloud account, between your own devices. We never see it.

For players

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. Nothing is collected, nothing is sold, and there is no advertising SDK to see you. Game Center is optional and is Apple's. Everything is a one-time purchase — nothing here renews.

The game speaks eleven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Greek, Ukrainian and Russian.
