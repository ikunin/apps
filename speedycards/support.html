Support

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, write to speedycards.support@gmail.com and say which device and iOS version you are on — it usually saves a round trip.

My Apple Watch unlock has not arrived.
The watch app is unlocked by the Apple Watch Edition purchase made on the iPhone. Open the store screen on the phone with the watch paired and tap Restore Purchases.
A duel link does not open the app.
Challenge links look like speedycards://duel?code=XXXXX-XXXXX. If tapping one does nothing, enter the code by hand: menu ▸ Duel ▸ Enter a code.
Does my progress follow me between devices?
Streaks and XP sync through your own iCloud, under your Apple Account, between your own devices. We cannot read it, and there is nothing to sign up for.
I bought something and it is still locked.
Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it is still locked, check that the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchases are Family Shareable, and Apple does not always propagate them instantly.
What is SpeedyCards Pro?
One purchase that unlocks the game on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. The Apple Watch app is a separate one-time purchase. Neither renews, and both are shared with your family group.
Is there anything my child could spend money on by accident?
Purchases go through Apple and require whatever authentication your device is set to ask for. If you want to remove the possibility entirely, use Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases and set In-app Purchases to Don't Allow. Everything already bought keeps working, and so does the free game.
Which languages does it speak?
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Greek, Ukrainian and Russian. It follows your device's language.
Does it need the internet?
No. The app works completely offline. A connection is needed only to buy or restore a purchase, because that is Apple's part of it.

Reporting a problem

Include the app version — it is at the bottom of Settings — along with your device and iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. If it is about a particular song, its name helps a great deal.

This is a small app maintained by one person. Replies are not instant, but every message is read.
