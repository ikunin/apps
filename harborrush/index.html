One move, and only one

Draw a line from a ship to its berth. That is the whole control scheme. Everything else is deciding which ship, and when.

Draw, don't steer

Trace a route with one finger and let go. The ship follows it exactly, at its own speed, while you deal with the next one.

Keep the lines apart

Two ships on crossing paths is a collision waiting to happen. Redraw either one and the crossing is gone.

Match the cargo

Every hull carries a colour, and only one berth will take it. Colourblind glyphs sit on top of the colours, not instead of them.

Seven regions, forty-nine harbors

From arctic ice to volcanic straits, each harbor is hand-tuned rather than generated: a fixed layout, a fixed set of hazards, and one right way to keep the traffic flowing that you have to find.

Try it free. The first harbors cost nothing, and the rest of the game unlocks in a single purchase — no advertising, no subscription.

The harbor fights back

Ice, mines and whirlpools

Icebergs drift across your routes, mines sit where you wanted to draw, and a whirlpool will take a ship off its line entirely.

Tides, currents, gates

Drawbridges and locks open on their own schedule. Currents push a hull off course by the time it arrives.

Pirates and shore cannons

Some harbors are under fire. You are still docking ships while it happens.

Race someone on the same deal

A duel gives both players the identical harbor and the identical ships, so the only variable is how you played it. Share a link or a short code and go — nearby or across the world.

There is no account and no sign-up. The challenge is a code you send over whatever app you already use.

For players

No ads. No tracking. No accounts. There is no advertising, no cross-app tracking and nothing to sign up for. Game Center is optional and is Apple's, not ours. Any usage counters are anonymous and cannot be tied to you — the privacy policy says exactly what is sent. One purchase unlocks the game, and it does not renew.

The game speaks eleven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Greek, Ukrainian and Russian.
