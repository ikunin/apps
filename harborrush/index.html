One finger. Every ship.

Touch a ship, draw its route to a berth of the same color and let go. That is the whole control scheme — everything else is deciding which ship, and when.

Draw a line

The ship sails your line exactly, at its own speed, while you deal with the next one. Change your mind? Draw a new line.

Unload, then send it back

Each hull unloads at the berth, then needs a second line back out to sea. The ships leaving are as dangerous as the ones arriving.

Keep them apart

Routes may cross; ships may not. Time them past each other, or reroute one. A single collision ends the shift.

Calm waters. Then the rush.

A shift starts with one boat and an empty harbor. Every delivery brings the next ship sooner — then two at once, then a freighter that holds a berth for six seconds while three skiffs circle.

That climb is the game: the moment where you are drawing lines with one eye on a collision you can see coming. It is a pleasant panic, and when it ends, Play Again is one tap away.

Forty-nine harbors. None alike.

Ice floes, reefs, locks, mines and volcanic coasts

The sea fights back

Pirates and shore cannons

Raiders chase the nearest ship. Aim the cannon from the beach and sink them before they drag a hull away.

A kraken

Tentacles rise out of the water and block a berth. Tap them back under.

Ice, tides and currents

Floes drift across your routes, low tide raises sandbars across the lanes, and currents push a hull off its line.

Locks and drawbridges

Gates open on their own schedule — or on yours, with a tap. Arrive at the wrong moment and you wait.

Bring a friend

Share one screen, or play nearby and online

Share one iPhone or iPad: each player owns the ships that enter on their side of the harbor, the berths change hands as the match goes on, and after three minutes the most cargo wins.

Or play on two devices — nearby, with no internet connection needed, or online against a Game Center friend or a random opponent.

Before you download

Is it really free?

Two harbors are free for good, with no ads and no time limit. The first is open straight away; the second opens after 25 units of cargo at the first. The rest of the game is a one-time purchase per region, or the World Pass for all of it.

Is there a subscription?

No. Every purchase is a one-time unlock that stays yours and restores on your other devices.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not to play solo. You need one to buy a region, for Game Center leaderboards and for online matches. Nearby matches work without the internet.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. You share one harbor, each player owning the ships from their side, for a three-minute match. Most cargo wins.
