What the app can and cannot get back

This app's purpose is to replace a video with a smaller one and then remove the original, so read this part. A video you picked from Files is kept in the app's own store for the period you chose, and you can put it back until that period ends. A video from your photo library is removed through iOS, which puts it in Recently Deleted for 30 days — that is iOS's window, not ours, and only you can restore from it. Once either period has passed, or if you delete the app, the original is gone and we cannot recover it. Nothing is ever deleted without you asking, and the app tells you what it is about to do; but the app is not a backup, and it is not a substitute for one.
