It finds them for you

Most of the space is in a handful of videos you have forgotten about. VideoSqueezer looks through the whole library and ranks every one by what it would give back — without encoding anything, and without changing anything.

Ranked, not listed

Sorted by the space each one would actually free, so the top of the list is where the space is. Sort by quickest win or by age instead if you would rather.

It says what it skipped

Videos already close to the quality limit are counted and excluded rather than quietly dropped. A list that omits things silently is indistinguishable from one that missed them.

Instant the second time

The result is kept, so reopening the screen shows the ranking straight away instead of scanning the library again.

And it is honest about what it saved

Compressing a video does not free any space. Deleting the original does. The history keeps those two apart: what was actually freed, and what is merely freeable while both copies still exist.

Free to try, at full quality. Videos up to 60 seconds are free forever, at every quality tier — not a reduced-quality demo. One purchase removes the length limit, and it does not renew.

You see it before you commit

Before and after, side by side

A short sample encoded from the busiest part of the video, where compression shows most. Drag to compare, hold to magnify.

A range, not a false number

The forecast is a range, because encoders overshoot and undershoot, and a single number would be precision the app does not have.

Trim, crop, and do it in bulk

Trimming costs nothing in quality. Cropping is a pinch over a live frame. Either can be applied to every imported video at once.

Two passes when you want them

Turn it on and the encoder studies the video before it encodes it, then spends the bitrate where the picture needs it. It takes longer. That is the trade, and it is yours to make.

Leave it running

iOS stops compressing about thirty seconds after an app leaves the screen, so a long queue cannot run in the background. Rather than pretend otherwise, there is a screen built to be left running.

One screen, hours of work

It holds the display awake, dims to a single line you can read from across a desk, and starts anything you had paused. It stops itself when the battery runs low or the phone gets warm, and carries on once it cools.

Locked while it runs

Face ID or Touch ID locks the app without stopping it: progress stays visible, thumbnails and titles do not. The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island can show progress alone — no picture, no filename.

For people who would rather not upload their videos

No uploads. No analytics. No account. Every frame is read, encoded and written on your own device. There is no advertising, no tracking and no server of ours to talk to — the privacy policy says exactly what stays where. One purchase, and it does not renew.
